## The convergent headline defect

All six frames' strongest-surviving objection landed on the **same** target: **the auto-executing kill-trigger — the one brake v0.4 trusts *because* it removes human willpower — runs on a single difficulty number set by the certification body *alone*, the one body with a survival stake in the brake never firing.** The vaunted three-body separation of powers therefore collapses to a **series system on one uncheckable variable**; "willpower is not the enforcement mechanism" fails because the willed hand simply moved upstream (score difficulty generously → the brake can never arm) and hid. The meta-audit was "thin," so no independent second estimate existed.

## The eight fixable-serious defects (→ v0.5)

1. **Endogenous auto-kill sensor** (the headline) — de-monopolize: externally-benchmarked, blinded, cohort-controlled difficulty requiring an independent second estimate to concur before the brake arms or is vetoed.
2. **Veto re-lets willpower in through the reasons box** — "auto-executing with a veto" is panel-executing with a default-on setting. Make core triggers non-vetoable; lesser vetoes delay-not-cancel + supermajority; repeated vetoes of the same trigger are themselves a kill-signal.
3. **Meta-audit is a single capture funnel** (quis custodiet) — make it public/adversarial (open data, published conflict register) or two-auditor co-signed; no interpretive monopoly.
4. **Refillable escrow = recurring recapture window; apparatus un-retirable while product is "optional"** — one-time alienated endowment or honestly name the gap; capped wind-down runway; a **mission-sunset protocol restoring Cato's authority to discontinue the *entire* Record** (never individual resolutions/panel composition).
5. **Appointment source left blank = capture point relocated and hidden** — name it; bind to multiple mutually-hostile external institutions; seat a Cato-hostile + a non-libertarian methodologist per body; publish the pool-overlap map; treat cross-body correlation as a capture gauge.
6. **Governance sequencing contradiction** — the Phase-1 gate is "panel-certified" but the panel isn't funded until Phase 2. Stand up a lightweight independent certifier from Phase 0; judge full independence before the gate.
7. **Protected axiom pole is uncapped/unmeasured/drift-exempt** — correct barbell design becomes a bloat/capture sink without a cap. Cap it ≤10–15% of Record spend; monitor the core-to-pole ratio.
8. **Gate-stack became proceduralism drift** — comprehensive falsifiability surveillance chills the high-variance bets it means to protect. Lightweight declining-to-card; direction-not-per-scholar-rate coverage; a gates-per-claim design ceiling; a falling-boldness alarm symmetric to the rising-CONFIRMED one.
