# The Cato Record — Product Specification (v0.5.1 — FINAL / terminal draft)

*Productization of Council Session 2 (2026-07-16), hardened through **four** adversarial rounds: the design-triad stress test (→ v0.2) and three hardcore-libertarian refinement rounds (→ v0.3 barbell, → v0.4 governance, → v0.5 terminal, → v0.5.1 final). The Round-4 terminal check ruled the **architecture terminal** and closed the loop (`stop-terminal`): the three surviving serious defects were one-edit, watcher-free scope-completions of v0.5's own fix set, applied here as v0.5.1. Everything remaining is an irreducible libertarian prior or a declared accepted floor — no refinement dissolves those. `convinces = false` (grudging worth-pursuing, never conviction) is the proven ceiling.*

> **The terminal picture (proven, not asserted, across four rounds).**
> - **Build:** the narrow, externally-scored core and nothing more — brief-to-ruling record; court *adoption* of Cato reasoning; economics read against markets/defaults/enacted text, not contested state statistics; the `WRONG IF` signature; misses shown first; institutional (not per-author) attribution; a non-enumerable registry; the blocking §0 legal gate; the statutory-reuse detector kept strictly internal.
> - **Never build:** any public affirmative influence / impact / "government we shrank" showcase. Dead in every framing tried (proximity, subtraction, and no fourth survived). The only public "we mattered" surface that lives is **court-adoption receipts**, under strict submission-framing at a granularity that yields no rankable/aggregable judicial-influence list.
> - **The ceiling on your bar:** the most hardcore libertarian reaches at most **grudging "the narrow externally-scored core is worth pursuing," held under permanent suspicion — never conviction** — because the product *is* the premise the purist rejects (a central scorekeeper of empirical truth). v0.4's governance build-out sharpened this rather than dissolving it: it made rule-*application* independent while rule-*authorship* (schema, thresholds, tier criteria) stays Cato's editorial judgment. This is owned in the open, not sold around.

## Governance-complexity ceiling (new in v0.5 — the via-negativa commitment)

Round 3's meta-finding: v0.4 got safer against shown objections and *more fragile* by bolting on machinery — each new mechanism is a new thing that must be defended, and "who watches the watchers" is an infinite regress. v0.5 therefore adopts a hard rule: **no mechanism may be added to defend another mechanism without removing an equal or greater surface, and where a defense would only spawn another watcher, the residual is declared an accepted irreducible floor (Residual Objections box) rather than patched.** Most v0.5 changes below are *subtractions*: caps, de-monopolizations, delay-not-cancel, and the restoration of a single blunt kill-switch.

## 0. Blocking legal gate (unchanged from v0.4)

No component documenting Cato's stated view on named legislation ships until outside tax/election-law counsel signs off in writing (501(c)(3)/§4911/501(h); LDA/state grassroots-lobbying; FARA; discoverability-as-admission). Top-line, costed like engineering. Every legislative-position lane routes through it; only pure judicial-act lanes carry the "external forum is scorekeeper" defense; counsel — not designers — decides which. If counsel cannot clear a component, it does not ship. The court-/data-scored core survives without any statutory-position machinery.

## 1. Product thesis (barbell — protected pole now capped, serious fix #7)

Cato adds a **verification layer to its empirical claims**, resolved by forums Cato does not control, surfaced as a queryable public record. A **barbell**:

- **Public safe core:** external, hard-to-capture receipts — *what we claimed, what happened, misses first* — narrated as **honesty**, never influence. Load-bearing: brief-to-ruling record and court adoption of Cato reasoning; economics against markets/defaults/enacted text.
- **Protected uncounted moral case:** its own surface, its own non-outcome justification, never scored as drift. **Now capped (fix #7):** the protected pole is a *real barbell tail* — bounded to **≤10–15% of total Record-related spend**, and the **core-to-pole spend ratio is a monitored, kill-gauged instrument** (not scored). **(v0.5.1 cosmetic):** "total Record-related spend" is a **named, audited cost base fixed at Phase 0 and frozen**, so the cap cannot be quietly widened by re-scoping the denominator. A funded lane with money and no metric is the textbook site of bloat: because it never shows a miss (it is never scored), Cato's incentive gradient would otherwise push prestige and headcount toward the lane that cannot embarrass it until the unmeasured pole swallows the measured core. The cap keeps the aggressive end small. *(The exemption itself stays — the purist's own prior, not a bug.)*
- **No middle:** no public affirmative showcase. Statutory-reuse is internal-only (§12).

**Scope, honestly:** the empirical slice only. **Optional infrastructure with a demand gate (§6).**

**Signature UI:** `WRONG IF <X> BY <date>`.

**Axiom exemption:** `NORMATIVE — not scored`, granted only after adversarial editorial review (written certification that no falsifiable corollary exists). Own surface, own capped budget (§1), never counted as drift.

## 7. Kill-criteria instrumentation (sensor de-monopolized #1; veto reconstituted #2; gate-stack right-sized #8; provisional baseline #11)

- **Ex-ante informativeness / difficulty (primary gauge, fix #1):** difficulty/base-rate **pre-registered at filing against an external benchmark**, under a **locked, versioned rubric**, **blinded from resolution outcomes**. The auto-kill (rising CONFIRMED-rate against **cohort-controlled** falling difficulty) fires on the concordance of the certification score, an **independent second estimate**, and an external base-rate/market proxy. **(v0.5.1 fix, F1/F20 — arming polarity corrected):** concordance is retained **only as the per-claim false-positive guard**; it is **not** the sole arming path. Because an AND-gate optimizes against false *positives* while the catastrophic tail here is the false *negative* (a captured scorekeeper nobody caught), and because certification — the body with a survival stake in the brake never firing — could otherwise suppress the alarm *passively* by simply refusing to see difficulty fall, a **second, disjunctive arming path** is added: **sustained, cohort-level, directional divergence of certification difficulty *above* the locked external proxy auto-arms the freeze** (the spec's own direction-not-rate / sustained-pattern logic from fixes #8/#9, applied consistently to the headline brake). Systematic difficulty-inflation now *trips* the brake instead of silently disarming it. The residual after this (sustained corruption of the external proxy itself) is the accepted small-pool / quis-custodiet floor and needs no further patch.
- **Falling-boldness alarm (new, fix #8):** carded-claim difficulty and long-horizon share, **symmetric to the rising-CONFIRMED alarm** — catches the chilling of high-variance bets, not just the farming of easy ones.
- **Coverage / direction (fix #8):** the coverage instrument detects **direction** — are donor-sensitive areas *systematically* under-carded? — **not per-scholar carding rates** (a productivity quota on falsifiability chills the contrarian bets a think tank exists to make).
- **Watcher health; external demand (with adversarial-valence tracked per §6); donor signal; resolution rate (~30% floor at 24mo); press weaponization.**
- **Mission-narrowing (fix #11):** issue-area diversity + long-horizon share, **quarterly**, against a **provisional baseline set at Phase 0** from Cato's existing pre-Record output mix (narrowing risk is highest in Phases 1–2); re-baseline against the provisional reference when the positional protocol goes live — never first-baseline from an already-drifted state.
- **Gate-stack ceiling (fix #8):** an **explicit design ceiling on total gates-per-claim**, so future rounds cannot keep accreting defenses (proceduralism is itself a drift).

**Enforced retreat (fix #2):** the two most consequential triggers (rising-CONFIRMED-against-falling-difficulty; internal-tool rising-`REVERSED`) are **non-vetoable** — the enforcement body may append public dissent but **cannot stay the brake**; any lesser veto is **delay-not-cancel** (the kill executes after a fixed cooling-off window unless ratified by a differently-sourced body/meta-audit), requires a **supermajority**, and **repeated vetoes of the same trigger are themselves a kill-signal.** §8 is restated honestly: enforcement is **a default-on rule plus public-only discretion — not the absence of discretion.**

**(v0.5.1 fix, F12 — the second non-vetoable brake de-monopolized symmetrically):** the internal-tool `rising-REVERSED` trigger must not read its rate off a curatable denominator. A per-entry `REVERSED` status is external fact, but `RATE = REVERSED / total` is gameable if `total` comes from the §12 tool, which §12 itself calls *curatable* — pruning durable entries or seeding fragile ones would move an **unstoppable** brake without faking any single classification (the v4 endogenous-sensor defect surviving on brake #2, upgraded by non-vetoable status into a capture-to-KILL weapon). Fix: **the `REVERSED` rate is computed off the immutable public resolution record, not the curatable internal tool**, and any §12 entries feeding this trigger are **frozen append-only for the duration of an armed window.** Same principle as fix #1, applied to both brakes.

## 8. Governance — structural independence, de-concentrated and correctable (fixes #1–#6)

- **Appointment source, named and adversarially heterogeneous (fix #5):** founding appointment is bound to **multiple pre-existing, mutually-hostile external institutions**, so no single source — Cato *or* any one adversary — can seat or purge a majority. **Each body seats ≥1 structurally-Cato-hostile appointer and ≥1 cross-ideological / non-libertarian methodologist.** The standing **pool-overlap / co-citation / shared-funder map is published**, and **measured cross-body correlation is itself a capture gauge** (Round-3: non-overlapping appointers from one small correlated pool is diversification on paper only — a series system under common-mode shock).
- **Separation of powers (three bodies + audit):** certification (difficulty/`principal_bet`/adoption) · enforcement (drift-freeze, non-vetoable core triggers) · attribution-grading (internal tool). **Meta-audit de-monopolized (fix #3):** it is **public and adversarial — published conflict register + open data auditable by anyone, no single official overseer to capture** (or, failing that, findings co-signed by two differently-sourced auditors); it **surfaces facts, it is not the sole judge of capture.**
- **Funding + mission-sunset (fix #4):** either a **one-time, irrevocably-alienated perpetual endowment** sized to self-fund the bodies, **or the spec states honestly that a true endowment is unaffordable and a refillable escrow is a recurring recapture window the design cannot yet close.** Escrow, if used, is **capped to a defined wind-down runway, not perpetuity.** A **mission-sunset protocol — distinct from panel-capture protection — restores Cato leadership's explicit, published authority to discontinue the *entire* Record** (never individual resolutions, never panel composition), which the panel administers/winds-down but cannot indefinitely veto. This is the single blunt kill-switch that answers "the apparatus is un-retirable while the product is optional." **(v0.5.1 cosmetic, F4/F21 — prospective-only wind-down):** the wind-down is prospective-only — **all claims already filed with a `WRONG IF` date, and any armed or in-flight kill-triggers, resolve and publish on their existing schedule *before* the sunset takes effect.** The switch stops *new* carding; it cannot suppress a pending resolution adverse to Cato. *(The deeper "threat-shadow" of a standing exit right — that a rational panel prices it in and self-censors upstream — is not patched: it is co-extensive with the irreducible independence-vs-correctability floor and is owned as such in the Residual box. Only this timing guarantee is fixable.)*
- **Rulebook published and versioned** as an object of external criticism; the meta-level is **openly owned as Cato's editorial judgment.** "Panel capturable over time" is instrumented (map + correlation gauge + public audit), **not solved** — see Residual box.

## Residual objections (the honest ceiling — irreducible priors + accepted floors)

**Irreducible libertarian priors** (owned, not fixable): (1) the state as economic scorekeeper; (2) state courts as arbiters — sharpened, because `VACATED`/court-adoption receipts *celebrate* a state organ's power; (3) a centrally-authored rulebook — **v0.4/v0.5 make rule-*application* independent, but rule-*authorship* stays Cato's, which is the purist's real objection**; (4) the subtraction frame still makes the state the reference frame ("liberty in the state's own units"); (5) the deepest — the greatest libertarian victory (an idea so absorbed no law cites it) is scored **zero by any registry, public or internal**; the honest terminal state of a maximally-successful Liberty Ledger is **an empty page.**

**Accepted irreducible floors (declared per the governance-complexity ceiling, not patched):**
- **Small-pool correlation floor.** The pool qualified to grade Cato's legal/economic claims *and* willing to sit on a niche verification panel is small and correlated; adversarial-heterogeneity appointment and the correlation gauge mitigate but cannot eliminate common-mode capture. Below this floor the risk is irreducible.
- **No fine-grained corrective for a drifted-but-not-dead panel — and its mirror, the mission-sunset threat-shadow.** Structural independence (escrow it controls, removal Cato can't initiate) means the only Cato-side corrective short of the whole-Record mission-sunset lever is coarse. Adding a finer corrective would only re-introduce the capture-by-Cato it was built to prevent — so the coarseness is accepted, not patched. The mirror of the same floor: because Cato *does* hold the blunt exit right, a rational panel prices in the *threat* of it and may self-censor upstream — the Round-4 most-escalated finding. Its only fixable slice (the prospective-only wind-down guarantee) is applied in §8; the residual threat-shadow is co-extensive with the irreducible independence-vs-correctability right itself (barring coercive exit while preserving legitimate exit would require a watcher to adjudicate legitimacy — regress — or would remove legitimate exit — self-defeat), and is owned here rather than patched.
- **The falsifiability instrument is Janus-faced.** Coverage/informativeness scoring can chill the very high-variance bets it means to protect; *dosage* is fixable (direction-not-rate, lightweight declining-to-card, gate-stack ceiling, falling-boldness alarm), the *existence* of the instrument is not — a residual chill is the price of the discipline.
- **Quis custodiet, terminally.** A public/co-signed meta-audit distributes the watch but cannot end the regress; at some depth "who watches the watchers" is answered by open data and adversarial appointment, not by another watcher.

The core is honest *about the slice it covers*; it is structurally silent about the victories that matter most. That is the ceiling.
